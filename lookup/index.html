    The street's color codes at a glance

    The same handful of colors mean different things depending on what they are painted on. Pick a system to decode it. The full explanations live in sections such as utility paint and hydrant colors.

    US / Canada utility locate colors (APWA code)

    Red

    Electric power lines, cables, conduit and lighting cables

    Orange

    Telecommunications, alarm or signal lines, cables or conduit

    Yellow

    Gas, oil, steam, petroleum or other flammable material

    Green

    Sewers and drain lines

    Blue

    Potable (drinking) water

    Purple

    Reclaimed water, irrigation and slurry lines

    Pink

    Temporary survey marks and unidentified facilities

    White

    Proposed excavation limits or route

    Source: Gopher State One Call · VA811

    NFPA 291 flow classes (US recommended practice; many cities differ)

    Light blue - AA

    1,500 gallons per minute or more

    Green - A

    1,000 to 1,499 gallons per minute

    Orange - B

    500 to 999 gallons per minute

    Red - C

    Under 500 gallons per minute

    Source: Mueller Co.

    US pavement marking colors (MUTCD 11th edition)

    Yellow

    Separates traffic going in opposite directions; left edge of divided roads and one-way streets

    White

    Separates traffic going the same way; right edge; most words, symbols and arrows

    Red

    Raised markers for truck escape ramps, or lanes you must not enter from the side where red shows

    Blue

    Supplements white lines at accessible (disability) parking spaces

    Purple

    Toll lanes for registered electronic toll accounts

    Green

    Bike lane and bike box surfacing

    Source: FHWA · FHWA

    US sign colors (MUTCD)

    Red

    Stop, yield, Do Not Enter, Wrong Way

    Yellow

    Warning signs (not temporary ones)

    Fluorescent yellow-green

    Pedestrian, bicycle and school warnings

    Orange

    Work zones and temporary traffic control

    Fluorescent pink

    Incident management: crashes and emergencies

    Green

    Guide and information signs

    Blue

    Traveler services and evacuation routes

    Brown

    Recreation and cultural points of interest

    Purple

    Electronic toll collection

    Black

    One-way, night speed limit and truck signs

    Source: trafficsign.us

    California Vehicle Code 21458 (other places differ or use none)

    Red

    No stopping, standing or parking

    Yellow

    Stop only to load or unload passengers or freight

    White

    Stop only to load or unload passengers or deposit mail

    Green

    Time-limited parking

    Blue

    Parking only for disabled persons and disabled veterans

    Source: California Public Law
